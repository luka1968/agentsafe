Version: 1.0 · Effective date: **October 21, 2026** · Operator: **workfern**

This policy is part of the AgentSafe Terms of Service (see

business rule.

## The refund rule

AI audit reports are virtual digital products generated instantly. Once
execution has completed successfully and the report has been generated and
delivered, the purchase is **non-refundable**. If a technical failure
results in an empty or missing report, the user is entitled to a **full
refund** upon contacting support.

No additional exceptions or promises beyond this rule are offered.

## How this maps to the payment flow

The x402 payment integration settles a payment only after the requested
audit work has executed successfully. Concretely:

- If execution fails, the payment is never settled: no funds move, and no
  refund is owed.
- If a payment settles but the report is empty or missing (technical
  failure), you are entitled to a full refund by contacting support.
- If the report was generated and delivered, the purchase is final.

## How to request a refund

Contact support at `lunanoach@gmail.com` with your task identifier and, if
available, the settlement transaction hash.
